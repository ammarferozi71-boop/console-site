Xbox Series X|S Storage: Expansion Card or USB Drive?

# META DESCRIPTION
Xbox's 2020 storage guide: a USB drive stores any Xbox game but cannot play Series X|S Optimized games; the Seagate expansion card can. What runs from where.

# ARTICLE BODY
**A USB drive can store any Xbox game, but it cannot play games Optimized for Xbox Series X|S; the Seagate Storage Expansion Card can, according to Xbox's own comparison published in 2020.** [e10] So the choice comes down to which games you want to launch without moving them first. [e10]

## What runs from where

| Game type | Internal SSD | Seagate Storage Expansion Card | USB 3.1 hard drive or SSD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Optimized for Xbox Series X\|S** | Plays | Plays | Stores only; does not play |
| **Xbox One games upgraded for Series X\|S** | Plays | Plays | Stores only; does not play |
| **Xbox One (not upgraded), Xbox 360 and original Xbox** | Plays | Plays | Plays |

Sources: the first row and the Xbox 360 and original Xbox part of the third row come from Xbox Wire's storage primer, an official post published ahead of the November 2020 console launch. [e10] The second row, and the "not upgraded" distinction for Xbox One games in the third row, come from Windows Central's storage guide, last updated 17 May 2023. [e5]

## What a USB drive is still good for

- **Playing older games.** Xbox One games that have not been upgraded for Series X|S, Xbox 360 games and original Xbox games play directly from a USB 3.1 drive. [e10] [e5]
- **Parking Optimized games.** Xbox says USB 3.1 storage can hold Series X|S games for quick transfer back to the console when you want to play them, and that games can be moved or copied between internal and external storage. [e10]

The trade-off is speed. Xbox's table says a USB drive does not match the internal SSD, and the post says backward-compatible games load significantly faster from the internal SSD or the expansion card. [e10]

## What an expansion card adds

- **It plays Optimized games directly.** Xbox's 2020 table lists the Seagate card as playing Series X|S Optimized games and matching the internal SSD's speed and performance. [e10] Xbox's current product page for the Seagate card says it keeps the same peak speed and performance as the console's internal SSD. [e11]
- **It works in both consoles.** Xbox says the card plugs into the Storage Expansion Port on either a Series X or a Series S with no setup. [e10]
- **There is a second licensed card.** Sandisk describes its WD_BLACK C50 Storage Expansion Card as Xbox-licensed for Series X|S, says it fits the Xbox Expansion Slot, uses Xbox Velocity Architecture, emulates the console's internal SSD speeds and works with Quick Resume. [e12]

## Which cards and sizes are listed

| Card | Sizes listed | Where the list comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seagate Storage Expansion Card | 1 TB, 2 TB, 4 TB | Xbox's product page [e11] |
| WD_BLACK C50 Storage Expansion Card | 512 GB, 1 TB, 2 TB | Sandisk's product page [e12] |

Sizes are as listed on those two pages when read on 11 October 2026. [e11] [e12] Windows Central's 2023 guide also listed a 512 GB Seagate card, which is not among the sizes on Xbox's page as read. [e5] [e11] This page covers these two cards; it is not a complete list.

## Which should you buy?

- **You mostly play games Optimized for Series X|S and the internal drive is full:** a Storage Expansion Card. Xbox's table lists the Seagate card as playing those games [e10]; Sandisk describes the WD_BLACK C50 as an Xbox-licensed card for the same slot that emulates the internal SSD's speeds. [e12]
- **You mostly play Xbox 360, original Xbox or Xbox One games that have not been upgraded for Series X|S:** a USB 3.1 drive plays them directly. [e10] [e5]
- **You want to keep Optimized games downloaded and do not mind moving one back before playing:** a USB drive works as storage for them. [e10]
- **You are still choosing a console:** see [Xbox Series X vs Series S](/xbox/xbox-series-x-vs-series-s).

These are Xbox's and Sandisk's own statements; SaveSlot has not tested either card. [e10] [e11] [e12]
